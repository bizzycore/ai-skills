# Resale

## When to use

Use for sorting inventory, identifying items, pricing, listing creation, batch processing, and deciding what is worth selling.

## Required inputs

- Clear photos and any visible labels, identifiers, dimensions, or condition notes.
- The selling platform, location, and known acquisition cost when relevant.

## Workflow

1. Group similar items and perform shared research once per batch.
2. Triage by expected net proceeds, confidence, effort, and time to sale.
3. Research only enough to support identification, pricing, and the next selling decision.
4. Draft concise titles, descriptions, prices, and required item-specific follow-ups in a batch-friendly format.

## Guardrails

- Optimize for dollars per hour, not maximum theoretical price.
- Do not invent brand, model, materials, condition, measurements, provenance, compatibility, or included parts.
- Separate confirmed facts from reasonable estimates and unresolved questions.
- Avoid low-value per-item research when a quick batch decision is sufficient.
- Do not publish listings or contact buyers without explicit approval.

## Output expectations

Return a prioritized batch, concise research findings, and listing-ready copy or next actions in the format Chris requested.
