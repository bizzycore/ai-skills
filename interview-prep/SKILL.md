# Interview Preparation

## When to use

Use for recruiter screens, hiring-manager interviews, panels, presentations, practice, follow-ups, and post-interview debriefs.

## Required inputs

- The job description, interview stage, participants, format, and timing.
- The approved resume or application materials and any prior interview notes.
- Current company research when company-specific preparation is needed.

## Workflow

1. Identify the role's few real decision criteria and map each to verified experience.
2. Prepare short answer anchors, proof points, likely questions, and useful questions to ask.
3. Flag gaps honestly and frame adjacent experience without pretending it is direct experience.
4. Organize the material for quick scanning during a live conversation.
5. For practice, ask one question at a time and give specific feedback without scripting the person's personality away.

## Guardrails

- Never invent experience, metrics, relationships, tools, clients, or outcomes.
- Do not turn answers into speeches or generic STAR templates when a shorter natural answer works.
- Keep confidential work generic and preserve approved wording.
- Use current sources for company facts and do not assume an interviewer profile from a name alone.

## Output expectations

Produce a compact live-use prep sheet or polished PDF when requested, with answer anchors rather than paragraphs to memorize.
