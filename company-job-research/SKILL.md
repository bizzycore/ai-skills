# Company and Job Research

## When to use

Use when evaluating a role, preparing an application or interview, or checking a company's market, product, leadership, funding, customers, and current priorities.

## Required inputs

- Company and role, plus the job description or posting URL when available.
- The decision or deliverable the research must support.

## Workflow

1. Start with the company's site, job posting, product documentation, filings, and other primary sources.
2. Use reputable secondary reporting only where it adds current context or independent evidence.
3. Separate confirmed facts, reasonable inferences, and unanswered questions.
4. Keep only findings that affect fit, positioning, interview questions, or a commercial decision.
5. Cite sources beside the claims they support and note material publication dates.

## Guardrails

- Never invent company facts, role requirements, contacts, customers, funding, metrics, or internal priorities.
- Do not repeat the company's marketing copy as analysis.
- Do not scrape gated personal data or automate outreach.
- Treat compensation, leadership, funding, and open-role status as time-sensitive and verify them live.

## Output expectations

Return a concise decision brief with key facts, implications, risks or unknowns, useful questions, and direct source links.
