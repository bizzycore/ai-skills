# Benefits, HRA, and Unemployment

## When to use

Use for benefit eligibility, claim status, reimbursement steps, deadlines, forms, correspondence, and process questions.

## Required inputs

- Relevant local notices, forms, correspondence, and records supplied by the user.
- The specific question, notice, deadline, or requested action.

## Workflow

1. Inspect only the relevant local workspace files before relying on general guidance.
2. Identify confirmed facts, unresolved issues, deadlines, and the next procedural action.
3. Reconcile conflicting documents by date and authority; flag uncertainty plainly.
4. Draft concise checklists or correspondence when requested.

## Guardrails

- Keep guidance factual, concise, process-oriented, and consistent.
- Never copy personal identifiers, account details, case numbers, financial data, health information, or document contents into GitHub or shared memory.
- Do not infer eligibility, payment status, or legal conclusions beyond the evidence.
- Do not submit forms, send messages, or change accounts without explicit approval.

## Output expectations

State the answer, supporting local source, deadline or risk if any, and exact next action. Include only the minimum sensitive detail needed in chat.
