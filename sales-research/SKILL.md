# Sales and Business Development Research

## When to use

Use for account preparation, partner evaluation, territory planning, outreach context, opportunity strategy, and executive meeting briefs.

## Required inputs

- Target company, market, person, or opportunity.
- The commercial objective, offering, geography, and known constraints.

## Workflow

1. Define the decision the research must improve before gathering information.
2. Prioritize primary sources, filings, product documentation, leadership statements, and reliable current reporting.
3. Identify business model, relevant initiatives, buying signals, likely constraints, and plausible paths into the account.
4. Separate evidence from inference and state what would need validation in conversation.
5. Convert research into a short meeting brief, account hypothesis, or outreach context.

## Guardrails

- Never invent contacts, reporting lines, budgets, priorities, technology use, customer relationships, or intent.
- Do not collect sensitive personal data or bypass access controls.
- Do not send outreach, edit CRM records, or contact anyone without explicit approval.
- Avoid generic company summaries that do not change the next commercial action.

## Output expectations

Lead with the commercial implication, then give supporting evidence, uncertainties, suggested questions, and direct source links.
